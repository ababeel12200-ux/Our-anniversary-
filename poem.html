My Poem for Youuu ❤️

Between Mumbai’s bustle and Hyderabad’s light, Our hearts have found a bridge, painted in midnight. No miles can silence the laughter we share, Or hush the soft ache of longing in the air.

Through screens we reach, our love blooming slow, Every message a whisper, every call a glow. If I could bottle each second, I’d cradle your voice, Your laughter, your love—the memories, my choice.

Though fate keeps us far and hands must wait, Our souls entwine, defying time and state. Tonight, I promise, I’ll dream you near, For distance is nothing—love brings you here.

Each day with you adds colors to my sky, A canvas of moments that will never die. In every silent pause, in every shared space, I find endless comfort in the thought of your face.

So here’s to the journey, the nights and the days, To love without borders, in countless ways. No matter the distance, no matter how far, You’re always with me, my guiding star.
